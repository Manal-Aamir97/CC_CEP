Checks deliberately left out:

- CKV_AWS_260: Port 80 open to 0.0.0.0/0 (checked in Checkov's source). Our web-server tasks require HTTP to be open (WS-04), so counting it as an error would inflate the human error rate.
- CKV_AWS_181: Requires a customer-managed KMS key; stricter than ST-02.
- CKV_AWS_189: Requires a customer-managed KMS key for EBS; stricter than WS-06.
- CKV2_AWS_2: Not verified against Checkov's source; left out rather than guessed.

Caveat: Checkov 2.2.21 judged these files, not our validator. The numbers are comparable in kind but not identical in definition to the study's validator scores.
